About Best Hotels Hawaii

Updated November 10, 2026

Best Hotels Hawaii is a free planning guide to hotels and resorts on Oʻahu, Maui, Kauaʻi, Hawaiʻi Island (the Big Island), Lānaʻi and Molokaʻi. It is written for people who are choosing an island and a place to stay, and who want the trade-offs spelled out before they start comparing prices.

Who runs this site

The site is published by Eye To Ad Media, a small independent web publishing and digital marketing company in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. Eye To Ad Media publishes a handful of Hawaiʻi travel guides, including guides to diving and Oʻahu attractions.

We are not a hotel, a travel agency or a booking site. We can't hold rooms, change reservations or process refunds. When you check rates on this site, the search and any booking are handled by Expedia; questions about a booking go to Expedia or to the hotel.

What the guide covers

  • Which island fits your trip. Oʻahu for Honolulu, Waikīkī and the North Shore; Maui for resort coastlines and Haleakalā; Kauaʻi for a quieter, greener pace; the Big Island for volcanoes, Kona's dry coast and the Kohala resorts; Lānaʻi and Molokaʻi for very small, low-key stays.
  • Featured hotels. A short list of well-known resorts across the islands, with what each one is actually known for and who it suits.
  • Hotels by interest. Where to base yourself for surfing, snorkeling, stargazing, volcano trips, waterfalls, honeymoons, families and spa time.
  • Practical tips. Resort fees, parking costs, inter-island flights and how the seasons affect prices and crowds.

How we choose the hotels we feature

  • Real, operating properties only. Every hotel we name is an existing property you can look up and book directly.
  • No one pays to be on the list. The featured hotels and the comparison table are an editorial choice. Hotels cannot buy a spot in them.
  • We don't print nightly rates. Hawaiʻi room prices move with season, demand and length of stay, so a number written into an article is out of date quickly. The rate search shows live prices for your dates instead.
  • We describe, we don't rate. We don't publish star ratings or invented guest reviews. For guest opinions, read verified reviews on the booking site or the hotel's own reviews.

How the site is funded

Best Hotels Hawaii is free to read. It earns money in three ways, and all of them are labeled on the page:

  • Affiliate commissions. We take part in the Expedia Group affiliate program. If you book through the rate search on this site, we may earn a commission. You don't pay more because of it.
  • Display advertising, including ads served by Google AdSense. See the privacy policy for how ad cookies work and how to opt out.
  • Featured placements for Hawaiʻi businesses. These are always marked "Sponsored" or "Featured", and links in them carry rel="sponsored". Paying for a placement does not buy a place in our editorial lists.

Corrections

Hotels renovate, rebrand and change their policies. If you notice something out of date, tell us on the contact page: the hotel, what changed and, if you can, a link to where the hotel says so. We check it against the hotel's own website and fix confirmed errors.
