Before you write: bookings go elsewhere

We are a guide, not a travel agent. We can't look up, change or cancel a reservation. If you booked through the rate search on this site, contact Expedia using the details in your confirmation email. For room requests, accessibility needs or the hotel's own policies, contact the hotel directly.
